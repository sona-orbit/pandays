Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Pandays needs no account and doesn't send your data to us. This page explains what data the app uses and where it is kept.

Your days and artworks

The days and artworks you create (pictures, stickers, numbers, layouts, colors) are stored on your device. With iCloud on, they sync through Apple's iCloud to your own iCloud account so your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch can show them. We cannot see this data.

Photos and AI pictures

Pictures you choose from Photos, paste or share into Pandays are processed only inside the app (cut into stickers, background removed, colors picked) and are never uploaded to us or to any AI service. You copy the prompts Pandays provides into ChatGPT or Gemini yourself; photos you upload there and pictures they create are covered by those services' own terms and privacy policies. Pandays only opens their app or website for you and never sends anything on your behalf.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Apple Watch read the same data, which moves between your devices (and your own iCloud) without passing through us.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone or iPad.

No account

Pandays has no account system. Your days, artworks and photos are not sent to us.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use information such as your device’s advertising identifier to show and measure ads, under Google’s privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). In regions where the law requires consent first, you are asked before any ads are shown. After you buy “Remove Ads”, no ads are shown.

Purchases

Payment for “Remove Ads” is handled by Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location based on your IP address. This applies only to the website, not to the app. You can block it in your browser's privacy settings or with Google's opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com
