Countdown app for iPhone and iPad

Your own character,on the best-looking countdown widget.

Pandays comes with 34 styles, from hand-printed characters like a grumpy pigeon in a raincoat to Creature Digits, where a character bends itself into the numbers. Pick one, let ChatGPT or Gemini turn your photo into a full picture, bring it back to Pandays, and it becomes your own stickers, numbers and widgets.

34 styles

One day, many ways to show it

8 styles are Creature Digits: the day count is spelled with 0–9 shaped from one character, and you can make a set from your own pet. 15 styles are sticker sets: each has an original character in 8 poses plus hand-drawn numbers. 11 styles are a single picture: one finished illustration that fills the whole widget. Every style lets you change the layout, the number font and the colors.

Make your own

Three steps to put your pet on a widget

In Pandays, a set of pictures of your own is called an “Artwork”. The hard part is already written as a prompt: you copy, paste and save the picture.

  1. 1

    Pick a style, copy the prompt

    In “Create”, pick a style and tap “Make My Artwork in This Style”. Choose who the star is (a pet, you, a couple, family or a small object), then tap “Copy Prompt”.

  2. 2

    Draw it in ChatGPT or Gemini

    Pandays opens ChatGPT or Gemini for you. Paste the prompt and attach a photo, and it draws one full picture. Without a photo, it draws the style's original character.

  3. 3

    Bring it back and use it

    Save the picture and send it to Pandays. A sticker set is cut into 8 poses and the numbers 0 to 9 automatically; a single-picture style uses the whole image as the background. Then drag, zoom, and change the layout and colors in the editor.

Where it goes

Where you look every day

  • Home Screen

    Small and medium sizes, plus a list of three days and a month calendar.

    From iOS 16, see how many days are left before you even unlock.

    A Watch app of its own, and complications for your watch face.

Count down or up

Days you are waiting for, and days you have come through

  • Count down to a date: an exam, a trip, a concert.
  • Count up from a date: being together, a pet coming home, quitting something.
  • “Count Up from Today”, or just “Count Down Days…”.
  • Repeat every week, month or year; birthdays can follow the lunar calendar.
  • Tap “+” for ten Templates; hide the ones you don't need in Settings.
  • Countdowns remind you the day before and on the day; count-ups on day 100, every 100 days and every full year.

Price

Every feature, free

Questions

Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. The 34 built-in styles work right away. To make your own, you only need to copy and paste: Pandays gives you the prompt, you paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini, save the picture and bring it back.

Do I have to pay for ChatGPT or Gemini?

You use your own account. How many pictures you can make and how fast depends on their current plans; Pandays doesn't charge for that part.

Where do my photos go?

Pandays never sends your photos to us or to any AI. You make the picture yourself in ChatGPT or Gemini, and their privacy policies apply there.

What if the stickers are cut badly?

In the artwork editor you can delete a bad sticker, add one of your own, or use the whole image as a single picture.

Which devices does it support?

iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 15 or later) and Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later). Lock Screen widgets need iOS 16 or later.

Will my days survive a new phone?

Days and artworks live on your device and in your own iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account with iCloud on, and they sync over.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every feature is free, and the app shows ads. To remove the ads, make a one-time purchase in Settings; it’s not a subscription. On a new device, use the same Apple Account and tap “Restore Purchase”.
