Support

If something doesn't work, or you have an idea, please write to us. Include your device model, system version and the screen where it happened (a screenshot helps), so we can find the cause faster.

Write to ussona.orbit.hi@gmail.com

Getting started

  1. In “Days”, tap “+”: pick a template, “Custom Day”, “Count Up from Today” or “Count Down Days…”.
  2. Choose an artwork: one of your own, or any built-in style.
  3. Add a Pandays widget to your Home Screen.

Making your own artwork

  1. In “Create”, pick a style and tap “Make My Artwork in This Style”.
  2. Choose the star, tap “Copy Prompt”, then open ChatGPT or Gemini.
  3. Paste the prompt, attach a photo (or don't), let it draw one full picture and save it to Photos.
  4. Back in Pandays, bring the picture in. A sticker set is cut automatically; a single-picture style uses the whole image.
  5. Adjust position, size, layout, numbers and colors in the editor, then save.

Tips for good pictures

  • Sticker sets cut most cleanly on a white background.
  • A photo where the subject is clear and not too small gives a closer likeness.
  • Delete a badly cut sticker in the editor, or add one of your own.
  • Single pictures look best with the subject on the right and space on the left, where the number goes.

Adding widgets

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit → Add Widget (on older iOS, the “+” in the top corner) and search for Pandays.
  3. Pick a size and place it. From iOS 17, touch and hold the widget to choose which day it shows.
  4. Lock Screen widgets need iOS 16 or later: touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap Customize.

Full screen

Open a day and tap “Full Screen”. Your upcoming days take turns, and the screen stays on; tap the screen to show the buttons. Set the seconds per day and the late-night dimming hours in “Settings”.

Removing ads and restoring

Every feature in Pandays is free, and the app shows ads. To remove them, make a one-time “Remove Ads” purchase in “Settings”; it’s not a subscription. On a new device or after reinstalling, use the same Apple Account and tap “Restore Purchase” in Settings.

Questions

Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. The 21 built-in styles work right away. To make your own, you only need to copy and paste: Pandays gives you the prompt, you paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini, save the picture and bring it back.

Do I have to pay for ChatGPT or Gemini?

You use your own account. How many pictures you can make and how fast depends on their current plans; Pandays doesn't charge for that part.

Where do my photos go?

Pandays never sends your photos to us or to any AI. You make the picture yourself in ChatGPT or Gemini, and their privacy policies apply there.

What if the stickers are cut badly?

In the artwork editor you can delete a bad sticker, add one of your own, or use the whole image as a single picture.

Which devices does it support?

iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 15 or later) and Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later). Lock Screen widgets need iOS 16 or later.

Will my days survive a new phone?

Days and artworks live on your device and in your own iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account with iCloud on, and they sync over.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every feature is free, and the app shows ads. To remove the ads, make a one-time purchase in Settings; it’s not a subscription. On a new device, use the same Apple Account and tap “Restore Purchase”.
