# Pandays (盼日) — countdown and anniversary widgets with your own character, for iPhone and iPad

Pandays is a countdown and anniversary app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Pick from 34 illustrated styles, or turn a photo of your pet, your partner or a favorite thing into your own sticker set with numbers, then put it on Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch widgets. Free, with every feature, and no account needed.

[Website](https://sona-orbit.github.io/pandays/) · [Guide](https://sona-orbit.github.io/pandays/guide/) · [Add widgets to your Home Screen and Lock Screen](https://sona-orbit.github.io/pandays/widgets/) · [Support](https://sona-orbit.github.io/pandays/support/) · [Privacy](https://sona-orbit.github.io/pandays/privacy/)

## Features

- **34 styles**: 8 Creature Digits styles where a character bends itself into the numbers, 15 sticker sets with an original character in 8 poses plus hand-drawn numbers, and 11 single-picture illustrations.
- **Make your own artwork**: Pandays writes the prompt for you; draw the picture with an AI image tool using your own account and a photo, bring it back, and it is cut into 8 poses and the numbers 0 to 9. Or skip AI and use your own photo as the background.
- **Poses change as the day gets closer**: far off, getting close, almost there, and the day itself.
- **Widgets everywhere**: Home Screen (small, medium, a three-day list and a month calendar), Lock Screen, and an Apple Watch app with complications.
- **Full Screen frame**: stand an old iPad or iPhone on your desk as a countdown display.
- **Count down or up**: exams, trips and concerts, or days together and a pet coming home. Repeat weekly, monthly or yearly; birthdays can follow the lunar calendar.
- **iCloud sync** across iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase; not a subscription.
