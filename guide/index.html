Draw your own artwork with AI

Every Pandays style comes with a prompt we have tested ourselves. Copy it, paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini, attach a photo, and bring the picture back to Pandays: it becomes a widget that is yours alone.

About five minutesNo drawing skills needed
→→PhotoAI pictureWidget

What you need

  • Pandays on your iPhone or iPad.
  • ChatGPT or Gemini (app or website). Free plans can draw too, but only a limited number of pictures a day.
  • A photo where the star is clear: a pet, yourself, the two of you, a favorite little thing. You can also skip the photo and describe it in words.

What you get

There are two kinds of styles:

1 / 4

Get the prompt in Pandays

  • Go to “Create”, pick a style you like and tap “Make My Artwork in This Style”.
  • Answer “Who's the star?”: a pet, yourself, a couple, a favorite thing, or family.
  • The page first shows what this exact prompt really produced. If you like it, tap “Copy Prompt”. The prompt is in English; paste it as it is, no need to translate.
  • Tap “Open ChatGPT” or “Open Gemini”.

2 / 4

Draw it in ChatGPT or Gemini

It works the same in ChatGPT and Gemini. The photo is optional.

  1. New chat, tap “+” to attach a photo
  2. Paste the prompt
  3. Send
  4. Wait a minute or two
  5. Share to Pandays, or save to Photos

ChatGPTIf it asks you a question first, reply “Please just draw it.”

GeminiIf it only answers in text, reply “Please draw it as one image.”

3 / 4

Bring the picture back to Pandays

  • If you shared it to Pandays, open Pandays and it asks which artwork to make.
  • If you saved it to Photos, go back to the same page and tap “Bring Back a Set” (for a single-picture style, “Bring the picture back”), then choose the picture.
  • A sticker set is cut into 8 stickers and the numbers 0 to 9 automatically; tap any bad cut to drop it.

4 / 4

Make it look the way you want

  • After saving, the editor opens: drag and pinch on the preview to place the picture, and change the layout, number style and colors.
  • You can always come back later: tap “Edit” on the artwork in “Create”.

What a good sticker-set picture looks like

123
  1. 1Row 1: four peeking half-body poses (flat at the bottom).
  2. 2Row 2: four full-body poses.
  3. 3Row 3: ten hand-drawn numbers, 0 to 9, left to right.
  • One square picture on a plain white background.
  • No other text, titles or watermarks.

When the picture isn't right

Reply in the same chat and ask it to redraw. One try is usually enough.

Numbers are missing, incomplete or out of order+
Reply:The bottom row needs ten numbers, 0 to 9, left to right. Please redraw.

If Pandays still can't cut ten numbers, that's fine: it shows the days in a font instead.

The background isn't white, or has a grid or shadows+
Reply:Make the background plain white with no shadows. Please redraw.

A white background cuts cleanest.

There is text or a title on the picture+
Reply:No text at all on the picture. Please redraw.
The eight poses don't look like the same character+
Reply:All eight are the same character with the same face, colors and clothes. Please redraw.
It doesn't look like my pet+

Use a clear, well-lit photo where the star isn't tiny, or add a line describing it at the end of the prompt, such as fur color or markings.

It drew several separate pictures+
Reply:Put everything in one single picture.

Pandays needs one whole picture.

It refuses to draw, or won't use a photo of a person+

Skip the photo and describe the star in words after the prompt, or try the other AI.

It says you've used up today's limit+

Free plans can draw only a few pictures a day. Try again tomorrow, or use the other AI.

You send your photo and the prompt to ChatGPT or Gemini yourself, and that service handles them under its own rules. Pandays never receives your photos and never uploads anything to us.
