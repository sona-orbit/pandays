Put your days on the Home Screen

Once your days are ready, put them on the Home Screen and see them every time you open your phone. Four steps, one minute.

1 / 3

Add to the Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the icons jiggle
  2. Tap Edit in the top-left corner → Add Widget
  3. Search for Pandays
  4. Swipe to pick a size and style, then tap Add Widget

2 / 3

Choose which day it shows

  • Touch and hold the widget → Edit Widget, then pick the day and artwork (iOS 17 and later).
  • On earlier versions it shows your nearest day automatically.
  • Widgets update at midnight, and right away when you change something in Pandays.

3 / 3

Add to the Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen
  2. Tap the widget area below the time
  3. Find Pandays and pick rectangular, circular or one-line (iOS 16 and later)

Other ways to show it

  • Calendar widget: medium size, showing the whole month or today plus this week.
  • Full screen: open a day and tap “Full Screen” to turn an old iPad or iPhone into a countdown frame.
